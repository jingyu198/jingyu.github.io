SYSTEM DESIGN

From training data to a walking robot

Data engine

Three sources, three needs

Open-source scenes supply 6.31 million step-level navigation samples. The site’s digital twin lets the model practise returning to a route after a mistake. Recordings on the X2 capture camera sway and the distance it actually walks.

MELIOR

From camera frames to motion

Both variants read the instruction and RGB images from the built-in camera. MELIOR-A predicts actions such as stepping forward, turning or stopping. MELIOR-N predicts a local navigation goal.

X-Navarch

From subtasks to the storefront

The planner breaks down the instruction and reviews progress at each stop. MELIOR follows the route. MELIOR-X handles the final approach to a named target. Obstacle avoidance guides execution, with LiDAR and odometry supporting target approach and low-level control.

Site adaptation in the lobby

In a digital twin of one office lobby, MELIOR-A navigated to vending machines in 100 held-out episodes. The bars show how often it finished within three metres of the goal.
